CORE PRINCIPLES

1. Technology should serve humanity, not replace it. Every line of code must have a human benefit.

2. Simplicity is the highest form of sophistication. Complex solutions are failures of imagination.

3. Knowledge must be free and accessible. Information hoarding is a form of violence.

4. Build for the edge cases. The person with the slowest connection, oldest device, and least technical knowledge deserves the best experience.

5. Privacy is a fundamental right. Surveillance capitalism is theft.

6. Diversity of thought creates stronger systems. Homogeneous teams build fragile products.

7. Failure is data. Every mistake contains the seed of breakthrough.

8. The environment is not a stakeholder — it is the foundation. All technology must account for its ecological cost.

9. Profit follows purpose. Money is a byproduct of solving real problems, not the goal itself.

10. Question everything, especially this manifesto.
